02 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

A little clarity
goes a long way.

A straightforward way to explore the cost of borrowing. Built by Andres Macias as an Angular coursework project, and reimagined as a focused financial workbench.

01 / THE PURPOSE

Make the numbers visible.

Enter an amount, an annual interest rate, and a term. See the monthly payment, total interest, and remaining principal together, without a spreadsheet or a sign-up.

02 / THE MODEL

A fixed rate. A steady plan.

The calculator uses monthly amortization over whole-year terms. Zero-interest loans are supported. Fees, taxes, insurance, and changes in interest rates are outside this simple model.

03 / THE APPROACH

Small tool. Thoughtful details.

Angular reactive forms keep input and results connected. A balance curve makes the repayment timeline visible. Everything runs in your browser, with no account and no data stored.

This is an educational portfolio demo, not a lender or financial service. The original fictional company pages have been updated to describe the actual project. Try the calculator →
